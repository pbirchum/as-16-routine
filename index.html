Plan Your Next VR Lesson

Pick a lesson you're actually planning to teach in VR in the next week or two. Walk through what it looks like when things go right, imagine what it looks like when they go wrong, and then map the specific admin-panel powers you'd turn on — and when — to keep the wrong version from happening. When you post, you'll see how your cohort is thinking about their own lessons.

1 · The lesson you're planning

What's the lesson? Give enough detail that a colleague could picture what students are doing — subject, grade, the VR room or app, the activity, roughly how long.

2 · What it looks like when it goes wrong

Now imagine the version of this lesson where everything that could go sideways, does. Be specific — what are students actually doing? What are you scrambling to fix in real time?

3 · The powers you'd turn on — and when

Now the plan. Which admin-panel powers do you turn on before class to prevent the wrong version? Which ones do you toggle during class, and at what moment? Be concrete — name the specific setting and the trigger.

4 · Post your plan to your cohort

Your plan will be composed into a single card and shared on the peer wall so others can see how you're thinking. You'll see everyone else's after you post.
